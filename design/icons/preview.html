Nana / icon system / four directions

Four ways to draw a quiet rabbit.

A new app icon and favicon system for Nana. Every direction is built at 16px first, then scaled up: one silhouette, two or three flat colours, and a mark that survives a circular mask. The mascot stays a rabbit; the notebook becomes a geometry cue rather than an illustration.

Palette Graphite, cream, one ember accent. No gradients.
Test surfaces Daylight paper #faf9f7 and low-lamp graphite #0c0c0d, the two Graphite canvases.
Recommendation

Ranked for the tab bar, then the home screen.

The first test is 16px in a tab on both paper and graphite chrome. The second is whether the mark still says Nana without the wordmark. Rabbit + note is selected. The shipped favicon drops the tile, crops the mark tightly, and switches between cream and ink paper with the app theme. The monogram remains the disciplined fallback if the rabbit ever feels too soft in real tabs.

01

Rabbit mark

best brand fit · strong 16px silhouette · safe mask

A solid head with two tall ears is still a rabbit at 16px, and it is the safest pure-16px silhouette. The ember inner ear is the warm detail; the silhouette does the work. It remains the fallback if the selected rabbit + note ever feels too soft in a real tab.

02

N monogram

best 16px clarity · best light/dark contrast

The paper tile and graphite N survive any surface, and the dog-ear keeps it from being a font sample. It is the safest technical choice, but it gives up the mascot and reads more like a publishing tool than Nana.

03

Rabbit + note

selected · transparent cropped favicon

The rabbit peeking over a note is the clearest one-sentence description of the product. The shipped favicon removes the tile and crops the mark so it fills the tab slot; the master keeps the same geometry for the app, sidebar, About, and public pages.

04

Note + board

most product-specific · weakest at 16px

Three columns plus one ember card says notes and boards without a document cliché. At 16px the fold disappears and the mark can read as a bar chart, so it works best as a secondary product glyph, not the app icon.

Method

What the small sizes are up against.

The working rules: design the tab icon at 16px, not 512px; reduce to one dominant silhouette; keep contrast against both paper and near-black chrome; use a full-bleed opaque background for maskable icons with the mark inside the central 80% circle; provide a single-colour alpha mark for themed/tinted contexts; and keep text out of the mark.
